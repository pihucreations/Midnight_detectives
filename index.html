💎 The Blue Diamond Mystery

🎮 How to Play

Your mission: Find out who stole Lady Celestine's Blue Diamond.

⬆️ ⬇️ ⬅️ ➡️ Move: Use the arrow buttons to walk around.

🚪 Doors: Walk onto a door to enter another room.

💬 People: Stand beside somebody and press Talk.

🔎 Objects: Stand beside an object and press Inspect.

📓 Notebook: Your notebook remembers suspects, evidence and mysteries.

🚨 Contradictions: If someone's story does not match the evidence, your notebook may spot it.

⚖️ Accusations: You need enough evidence before you can prove who did it.

Important: Explore carefully. Some clues only make sense after you talk to someone!
